<p>Merovelis AI builds and operates AI software products. <a href="/products#strategos">Strategos</a> is the situation room for logistics and critical operations. Verentir evaluates AI systems, beside the Thalamus and TruthMetal platforms. <a href="/products/robson">Robson</a> is an open-source execution and risk engine.</p>
<h2>The common base</h2>
<p>The products share identity, content, payments and operations, on infrastructure that Merovelis runs. Thalamus controls AI calls through policies, routing and audit. TruthMetal holds the reference datasets and criteria for evaluations. Each product states its own stage in the <a href="/products">portfolio</a>.</p>
<h2>Sectors</h2>
<p>Logistics is the sector of Strategos and one the house cares most about. Merovelis is not limited to it: the other products serve crypto markets, subscription content, AI quality and education.</p>
<h2>Social responsibility</h2>
<p>Olha Escola is the Merovelis social project, under the signature Cidadania na Escola (citizenship at school). It is in preparation and has not launched. It is not a commercial product.</p>
