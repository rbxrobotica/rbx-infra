<h2>From RBX Robótica to systems engineering</h2>
<p>The company began as RBX Robótica, a consultancy focused on automation, robotics and custom software. Its early projects had a direct objective: remove repetitive work and connect technology to concrete operational problems.</p>
<p>The scope grew with the problems. Automating one isolated task is rarely enough when data, people, integrations and decisions must keep working together. The company, by then RBX Systems, expanded into backend engineering, cloud infrastructure, integrations and complete operational platforms.</p>
<h2>The move to governed systems</h2>
<p>The adoption of language models and AI agents raised the standard. Producing a plausible answer was not enough. Quality had to be measured, costs controlled, decisions recorded, actions bounded and people kept accountable for outcomes.</p>
<p>That need shaped the work of the house: AI engineering, LLMOps, operational automation, observability, sovereign infrastructure and open-source products for operations that demand control.</p>
<h2>From RBX Systems to Merovelis AI</h2>
<p>In November 2026 the company took the name Merovelis AI. Until then Merovelis was the house’s product brand for logistics. With the change, the name belongs to the whole company, and products come before services: Merovelis builds and owns the systems it operates.</p>
<h2>What we build today</h2>
<p>Merovelis builds and operates its own products: Strategos for logistics and critical operations, Robson for execution and risk, Satwake, Verentir and Initelo, on an AI platform with Thalamus and TruthMetal. The same team serves a limited number of clients through the engineering partnership.</p>
<p>The portfolio is proof of work. Each product starts with a real operational problem and must become observable, auditable and recoverable before we consider it complete.</p>
<h2>What has not changed</h2>
<ul>
<li>Solve real problems before following trends.</li>
<li>Design for operation, maintenance and failure, not only the first release.</li>
<li>Keep evidence and human accountability around consequential decisions.</li>
<li>Prefer understandable systems over black boxes that are hard to govern.</li>
<li>Record what was decided, why it changed and how to recover.</li>
</ul>
